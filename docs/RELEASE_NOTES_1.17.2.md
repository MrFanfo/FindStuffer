# Findstuff 1.17.2

Places and categories open their branches in the list again, and remember how
you like their items shown. No database changes: upgrading from 1.17.1 needs
no migration.

## Places and categories

- **Tap an icon to open its branch.** A place or category with anything inside
  opens it right in the list when its icon is tapped, with the children
  indented beneath it; tap again to close it. A small arrow on the icon shows
  there is something to open and turns when it is open. Tapping the name still
  opens the place or category itself.
- **Change icon** is now in each row's ⋯ menu. The category menu closes after
  a choice, like the place menu.
- **Grid or list, and the sort, are remembered.** The choice made inside any
  place or category applies to all of them and survives closing the app.
